How the automatic analysis works

The app downloads up to 10 years of available daily history so it can show up to five years and use earlier data for model fitting. It compares market and sector funds, gold and oil proxies, Treasury yields, oil prices, volatility and currency where available. It also evaluates past-only price baselines, lagged response models and regression models. Each eligible line must cover the full selected window and is ranked by root-mean-square distance from the stock on those dates, as a percentage of the first displayed price. A source that has not published the whole range is excluded rather than filled in. The three lowest-error lines are shown. A price-derived line can rank highly simply because it uses the stock’s own past prices. The historical shape match selects and scales a past segment using the entire displayed period (hindsight). A high match does not establish a cause.

Some PDF techniques produce event intervals, diagnostics, or scores rather than price lines; others need unavailable intraday, company-network, news-attention or factor data. Those are not silently claimed as implemented. The selected lines are not a forecast or investment advice. Models using same-day market values are descriptive, not forecasts.

Data sources & limitations

U.S. ticker directory: Nasdaq Trader, with Twelve Data as fallback. Adjusted prices and market/sector proxies: Yahoo Finance’s unofficial public chart endpoint. Economic data: FRED, with clearly labeled Yahoo market proxies if FRED fails. Browser requests use public relays (Jina Reader and cors.dev), which can see requests and may cache, rate-limit, fail or change without notice. Yahoo’s data is intended for personal use; check its terms before deployment. Sector funds are general proxies, not verified company peers. FRED data may be revised and dates do not necessarily represent publication times. The Fama–French factor dataset from the earlier server version is not available in this static version. Missing feeds reduce the candidate set. Prices and results are held in browser memory during the session.

This static version works on GitHub Pages without your own server, but depends on external data providers and a public relay. Source failures are shown; there is no guarantee of full historical coverage or provider availability.
