Formula notes & limitations

Know what each statistic does—and what it cannot establish.

METHOD

Market model & abnormal returns

Fit Rᵢ = α + βRₘ + ε on observations before the chart window; calculate predicted return and abnormal return in the displayed period. The residual is relative performance under this chosen benchmark—not the share of movement caused by a specific event. A different benchmark, estimation window, split adjustment or confounder changes the answer.

Peer, macro and multiple regression

Equal-weight basket uses the mean of your selected daily returns. Multiple regression estimates ordinary least-squares coefficients for selected comparator returns. Correlated predictors, short histories, data gaps and look-ahead make coefficients unstable. The interface requires a pre-window training sample and reports out-of-sample fit when possible.

News and timing tests

GDELT indexes coverage; tone is an automated text signal, not a truth or investor-belief meter. Matched days, cross-correlations and news volume are exploratory. Timing can be noisy; headlines can be published after a move, and one story can create many articles. News results have a limited recent lookback.

Limits of correlation & causal language

Correlated lines may share a cause, trend together by chance, or reverse in another period. Scanning many assets/lags increases false discoveries; p-values here are unadjusted descriptive screens, not confirmation. Stronger attribution needs an independently verified event, suitable counterfactual, correct timing, alternative explanations, robustness checks and ideally out-of-sample replication.

Research basis & data sources

Useful methods, their provenance, and the constraints of this HTML-only version.

Expected-return models & abnormal-return equations ↗Market model: estimate alpha/beta in a pre-event estimation window, then calculate abnormal returns and CAR. Kenneth French Data Library: Fama/French factors ↗Market, size, value, profitability, investment and momentum factor data. This prototype does not claim to calculate the formal FF5 portfolio factors. GDELT DOC 2.0 overview ↗Global news article discovery and aggregate timelines; recent-window coverage only and not a complete news archive. News impact & financial sentiment research (Li et al.) ↗Research compares financial text representations; sentiment is noisy, and a polarity-only score can be inadequate. SEC EDGAR search ↗Primary-source verification for company filings. Follow key event claims to the issuer filing/release, not just a news summary.
This browser-only app does not store company universes, API keys, searches or fetched data in local storage or a server. Requests go to the configured third parties. A production-grade product would need a secured backend/data license for dependable multi-symbol history, full-market peer discovery, shared workspaces and scheduled refresh.
